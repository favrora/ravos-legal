Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

This policy explains how the Ravos team ("Ravos", "we", or "us") processes information in the Ravos mobile application. Contact us at webcoder.ru@gmail.com.

Information you choose to provide

When you request writing help, you may provide conversation text, a draft, profile details, bio facts, and optional examples of your writing style. Ravos sends the text you reviewed to our protected backend and AI processor only when you tap Generate. Do not submit information you do not have permission to process.

Screenshots and local OCR

Selected screenshots are processed on your device to recognize text. Ravos does not upload screenshot image files in version 1. Extracted text is editable, roles can be reviewed, and common personal information can be redacted before an AI request.

Account, purchase, and technical data

Ravos uses a pseudonymous Firebase identifier for authentication and quotas. You can continue as a guest. If you choose Login with email for an existing account, Firebase processes your email address and password to authenticate you; Ravos does not save your password on the device. The app does not offer account registration. Apple, Google, and RevenueCat may process store account, purchase, subscription, device, and diagnostic information under their own terms. Our service may process privacy-safe operational data such as request ID, app version, response status, latency bucket, and usage counts. We do not use message text for advertising.

Purposes and processors

We process data to provide AI suggestions, protect the service, enforce limits, verify subscriptions, respond to support, and diagnose failures. Service providers may include Apple, Google Firebase and Google Cloud/Gemini, RevenueCat, and hosting infrastructure configured for Ravos.

Storage and retention

Saved chats, generated suggestions, voice examples, and preferences are stored locally until you delete them or remove the app. Server-side idempotency records are short lived, entitlement cache is temporary, and privacy-safe webhook metadata may be retained for up to 90 days. Legal, fraud-prevention, and security records may be kept longer where required.

Reports about AI-generated content

On Android, you can report an AI answer without leaving Ravos. After you confirm, we receive the three original generated suggestions, your selected reason, generation and report identifiers, writing mode, app version, and a pseudonymous reporter identifier. A report does not attach your input chat, screenshot files, writing examples, or edited draft. Generated text may itself repeat details you provided. We use reports to review safety issues and improve safeguards, not for advertising. Reports are scheduled for deletion after 90 days, unless a longer period is required for a legal or security investigation. Contact support to request earlier deletion.

Your choices

You can decline AI processing, edit or redact text, avoid saving a chat, disable reminders, delete local data in Profile, or request deletion of server-side data. See Request deletion of Ravos data for the steps, data covered, and retention exceptions. Deleting local data or a Ravos account does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription; subscriptions are managed through the relevant store account.

Age and safety

Ravos is for adults aged 18 or older and is not intended for minors. We may block requests involving minors, coercion, harassment, threats, stalking, or sexual exploitation.

International processing and security

Processors may handle information in countries other than your own. We use HTTPS, authenticated access, App Check, validation, quotas, and data minimization, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy when the product or applicable rules change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
